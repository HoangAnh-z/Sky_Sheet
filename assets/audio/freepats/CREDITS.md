# FreePats recordings

- Upright Piano KW, version 2022-02-21. Recorded by Gonzalo and Roberto for FreePats. [Original source](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html#UprightKW)
- Spanish Classical Guitar, version 2019-06-18. Recorded and prepared by Roberto for FreePats. [Original source](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Guitar/acoustic-guitar.html)

Both sources publish these recordings under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). See [the included CC0 text](CC0.txt). Sky_Sheet_Piano selects individual notes and encodes them as MP3, with peak adjustment and a short fade where each sample ends. Full provenance and build instructions are in [AUDIO-SOURCES.md](../../../docs/AUDIO-SOURCES.md).
